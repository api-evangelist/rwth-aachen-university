RWTH is currently reorganizing how its subsidiaries handle our knowledge transfer strategy with the aim of effectively harnessing our potential in this area. RWTH Innovation GmbH will become RWTH Aachen Campus Innovation GmbH, and it will be responsible for the main content-related aspects of the RWTH Aachen Campus in the future. This will solidify its role as the central point of contact for companies in all knowledge transfer matters.
